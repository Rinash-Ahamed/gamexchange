## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production artwork for the Our presence section of GamexChange's gaming-store franchise website. Create a premium polished landscape 3:2 illustration: four elegant futuristic circular glass-and-metal network hubs on a deep navy blue background, linked by restrained cyan and violet luminous fiber-optic paths. One slightly larger primary hub represents Coimbatore and three smaller hubs represent Pollachi, Perundurai, Karur. Sophisticated gaming technology aesthetic, clean spacious composition, crisp subtle dimensional materials, controlled cyan and magenta accents, not busy cyberpunk clutter. Include exactly four large clean highly legible city labels, each adjacent to its hub: 'COIMBATORE', 'POLLACHI', 'PERUNDURAI', 'KARUR'. No other text, numbers, slogans, logos, device brands or watermark. This is an abstract schematic network, NOT a geographically accurate map: no geographic outlines, no street maps, no invented roads, addresses, pins, directions or distances. Entire illustration and every label fully contained within a generous 12 percent safe margin on all sides; no cropped hubs or edges; designed for full-image object-contain display beside an HTML list of the genuine locations. Attractive both on light and dark themed page surfaces. Opaque deep navy background.
